Adhyat – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Adhyat is a discourse audio player. It does not require an account and does not collect, store or sell any personal information.

Data on your device

Playback position, favourites and any audio you choose to save for offline listening are stored locally on your device only. Clearing the app's data removes them.

Network requests

The app loads its catalogue from our server and streams audio through it from oshoworld.com. Standard server logs (such as IP address and requested URL) may be kept temporarily by our hosting provider for operation and security. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
